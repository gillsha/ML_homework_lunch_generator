    How the comparison works

    Each movie has 18 binary genre features. Your profile is the unweighted arithmetic mean of those raw features: a genre present in two of three films has weight ⅔. We compare vectors using cosine similarity and exclude every film in your history from both lists.

    Scores are similarities, not probabilities of liking a movie. This is genre-based recommendation, not collaborative filtering or a trained model. MovieLens rating counts are context only; popularity and average ratings never affect ranking. Equal scores use a fixed, deterministic ID hash.
